Scale cards for the daily scouting Print this page at 100 % (Actual size), not "fit to page", on a colour printer. Check one card with a ruler: the magenta square must be 5.0 cm on each side and the ruler 8 cm long — if not, the printer scaled it and the sizes will be off. Cut, laminate, and keep one in each scouting kit. In a growth photo, put the card flat beside the fruit or leaf, at the same distance from the phone.
