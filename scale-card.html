Scale cards for the daily scouting Print this page at 100 % (Actual size), not "fit to page" — black and white is fine. Check one card with a ruler: the black square must be 5.0 cm on each side and the ruler 8 cm long; if not, the printer scaled it and the sizes will be off. Cut along the dashed lines, keeping the white border round the square, laminate (matte if you can — a shine on the square hides it), and keep one in each scouting kit. In a growth photo: the card flat beside the fruit or leaf, at the same distance from the phone, the whole square in the picture. The old magenta cards are no longer used.
